## [Download Kinoklip 1.1.1 Beta for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v1.1.1-beta.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with Gear Lever (or update it there), or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Beta: keep backups.

**New to Kinoklip? Read the [guide](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/guide/readme.md).**

### New
- **Shortcuts from your old editor**: keyboard presets for **Vegas Pro** (S splits), **Premiere Pro**, **Final Cut Pro**, **Avid Media Composer**, **Kdenlive** and **Shotcut** in Keyboard Customization, and import of Premiere `.kys` and Final Cut `.commandset` files. On first start, the Project Manager asks "Coming from another editor?".
- **Render finished notification**, like HandBrake: "Render complete" (or what failed), with Open Folder and Play. Turn it off in Preferences > User > UI Settings.
- **Drag files from your file manager onto the timeline**: a ghost clip shows the file's real length on the track under the cursor, snapping like a Media Pool drag, and the clip lands where you drop it.
- **Bins show up as folders** in the Media Pool, as in Resolve: open them with a double-click, drop clips on them, rename and delete them there.
- **Sort by Type** in the Media Pool (and a Type column in list view).
- **Choose where imports go**: the bin you are looking at (default) or always the Master bin, in Preferences > User > Editing.
- The long **View menu** is grouped into Switch to Page, Show Panel in Workspace, Viewer and Viewer Overlays.
- **Title presets** in the Effects library, including scrolling credits; title Position and Anchor in the Inspector.
- **HDR phone videos** (HLG / PQ) look right on SDR timelines instead of washed out.

### Fixed (thanks to the testers)
- Deliver now picks **In/Out Range** when only an In or only an Out mark is set, and no longer switches your choice back to Entire Timeline when a mark moves.
- The Metadata panel's Clip Details values were invisible with the Breeze style.
- Clear messages for broken or unsupported files, including truncated MP4 recordings.
- Selecting a title opens the Title tab; dropping onto the empty area below the tracks makes a new track; frame-rate conversion rounds clip ends correctly.

Projects saved by this version (format 17) cannot be opened by versions before 1.1.0.
