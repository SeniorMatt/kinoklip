Kinoklip 0.3.0-alpha.1 - first v3 alpha ("polish basic editing"): audio while shuttling and scrubbing, an edit overlay on the timeline viewer, a two-up trim view, typed timecodes, and the new Audio page. Still an alpha: expect bugs, keep backups.

## Download and run (Linux x86_64)
1. Download `Kinoklip-x86_64.run`.
2. Make it executable: right-click > Properties > "Is executable", or `chmod +x Kinoklip-x86_64.run`.
3. Double-click it (or run `./Kinoklip-x86_64.run`). The first start unpacks into `~/.cache/kinoklip`; later starts are fast.

Needs: x86_64 Linux, OpenGL 3.3, `zstd` + `tar` (or `bsdtar`). Wayland and X11. `kinoklip-x86_64.tar.zst` is the same app as a portable folder; `SHA256SUMS.txt` has checksums.

## Editing polish (v3)
- **Hear the audio while shuttling**: J / L at 2x - 8x, reverse and slow play (K+J / K+L) play sound with the pitch following the speed, like a tape.
- **Audio scrubbing** (Shift+S, on by default): dragging the playhead or stepping frames plays short snippets.
- **Edit overlay**: drag a clip from the media pool or the source viewer onto the timeline viewer and drop it on Insert, Overwrite, Replace, Fit to Fill, Place on Top, Append at End or Ripple Overwrite.
- **Two-up trim view**: while trimming, rolling, slipping or sliding, the timeline viewer shows the frames on both sides of the edit with their timecodes.
- **Typed timecode**: with the timeline focused, type digits and press Enter to jump there; a leading `+` / `-` moves the playhead, or the selected clips / edit point, by that amount.
- **Offset label** next to the pointer while moving, trimming, rolling, slipping or sliding.
- Alt+Up / Alt+Down move the selected clips one track; Change Clip Duration in the clip menu; Delete Empty Tracks in the track header menu; Replace (F11) lines up the source viewer's playhead with the timeline playhead when the source has no in mark.

## Audio page (Shift+7)
- Audio tracks shown tall with waveforms, level lines and fades; meters in the track headers.
- A mixer with a strip per track and per bus: 6-band EQ and compressor / limiter editors, pan, fader, mute, solo, bus routing. Add, rename and delete buses; choose the bus that is rendered (Bus 1 by default). The Edit page's Mixer panel shows the same strips.
- Fader and pan automation (Off, Read, Touch, Write).
- Peak meters and a loudness meter (EBU R128: momentary, short-term, integrated LUFS, true peak).
- Playback, scrubbing and export sound the same.

## Notes
- Projects are saved in a new format version; projects from 0.2.x open unchanged, but older Kinoklip versions cannot open projects saved by this one.
- Audio scrubbing can be switched off with Shift+S (Timeline menu).

## Known limitations
- Track names on the Audio page's track headers are cut short.
- Shuttle sound at 4x - 8x is slightly harsh (simple resampling).
- The two-up trim view shows the clips' own frames, without effects.
- Recording, plugins and surround formats on the Audio page are not implemented.
- Colour grading and compositing pages are planned for v4.
- Intel VAAPI paths are still unverified on real Iris Xe hardware.
