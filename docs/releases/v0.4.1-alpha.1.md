## [Download Kinoklip 0.4.1 for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v0.4.1-alpha.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with [Gear Lever](https://flathub.org/apps/it.mijorus.gearlever), or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Alpha: keep backups.

### New
- **Zoom the viewers** with the mouse wheel; middle-drag (or Space + drag) to pan, Shift+Z to fit.
- **Import a whole folder**: File > Import Media Folder, or drag a folder onto the Media Pool. Subfolders become bins.
- **Audio transitions**: six crossfades in the Effects library.
- **Markers**: clips snap to clip markers; M on a marker removes it.
- **Alt-drag** copies a clip with its linked audio and its transitions.
- **Guides snap** while you hold Ctrl.
- The Deliver page uses your **In / Out range** automatically.
- Kinoklip now comes as one **AppImage** file.

### Fixed
- Ctrl+Z could stop working.
- Audio stayed on the speakers after connecting headphones (AirPods).
- Media Pool Delete did nothing on a clicked thumbnail.
- An old picture could show in a gap after editing inside a compound clip.
- The Render Queue progress bar stayed empty.

Projects are compatible with 0.4.0.
