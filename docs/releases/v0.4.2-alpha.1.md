## [Download Kinoklip 0.4.2 for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v0.4.2-alpha.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with [Gear Lever](https://flathub.org/apps/it.mijorus.gearlever), or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Alpha: keep backups.

**New to Kinoklip? Read the [guide](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/guide/readme.md).**

### New
- **A user guide** with step-by-step articles and screenshots: getting started, export for YouTube, remove a green screen, change clip speed, fade audio and add music.
- **Help inside the app**: press **F1** to open the article about what you are working on; a small **?** appears next to panels and effects that have one; **Help > Guide** lists and searches every article.
- **Z** resets the zoom of the viewer under the mouse.

Projects are compatible with 0.4.0 and 0.4.1.
