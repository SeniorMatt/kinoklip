## [Download Kinoklip 1.1.0 Beta for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v1.1.0-beta.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with Gear Lever, or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Beta: keep backups.

**New to Kinoklip? Read the [guide](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/guide/readme.md).**

### New: the Color page (Shift+6)
- Grade every clip, or the whole timeline, with **nodes**: serial, parallel and layer (Alt+S, Alt+P, Alt+L), each with a live thumbnail.
- **Primaries** wheels and bars, **Curves** (custom and the five HSL curves), **Qualifier** with highlight, **Windows** (circle, linear, polygon, gradient) with on-screen handles, and **LUTs** (.cube).
- **Gallery** stills (Ctrl+Alt+G), apply a grade to other clips, split wipe, and copy grades from the clips strip.
- **Scopes**: waveform, parade, vectorscope and histogram, also as a floating window on any page (Ctrl+Shift+W).

### Also new
- **Composite Mode** in the Inspector: 19 blend modes (Screen, Multiply, Overlay and more), also on the Compositing page's Merge node.
- Timelines now start at **01:00:00:00** and new projects default to **24 fps**, as in Resolve (older projects keep their settings).
- **11 new guide articles**: fade to black, freeze frame, picture-in-picture, smooth playback, vertical video, blurring a face, subtitles, keyframes and more. Press F1 in the app.
- Put a **logo over a clip** on the Compositing page: drag a picture from its new Media Pool panel onto the node editor.
- Double-click a timeline clip to open its source; double-click a track name to rename it; Shift+Z toggles back to the previous zoom.
- **Create New Timeline** dialog (start timecode, name, track counts); new timelines start with V1 and A1, as in Resolve.
- **Edit a queued render job** with the pencil on its card, then Update Job.
- **Resolution presets** in Timeline and Project Settings; **Replace Selected Clip** for a file that was renamed.
- 3 more guide articles for the Color page: colour correct a clip, change the colour of one object, use a LUT.
- **Dynamic Zoom** (with on-screen start and end boxes) and the **Speed Change / Retime** sections in the Inspector.
- The **menu bar** now follows Resolve: a Kinoklip menu (About, Preferences, Keyboard Customization, Quit), File > Import, Workspace.
- The first import asks to **match the project frame rate** to your clips, as Resolve does.
- Trimming: `,` and `.` slip and slide in trim mode, dynamic trim (W) on selected clips, a Dynamic Trim button, Up / Down move a selected edit point.
- Marker and flag colour buttons on the timeline tool bar; captions follow their text and go on the subtitle track you right-click.

### Fixed (many thanks to the testers)
- Menus and drop-downs now open instantly, without fading.
- Audio scrubbing only plays when dragging the playhead from its head.
- Delete in the media pool or another panel no longer deletes timeline clips.
- Position Y now counts up, as in Resolve; the Inspector stays on the Video tab after edits; arrow keys work in focused lists, fields and drop-downs.
- Alt+V (Paste Attributes), Alt+F and Alt+M work; messages say "1 clip" instead of "1 clip(s)".
- Changing the frame rate of a timeline that already has clips is no longer possible (it made clips play at the wrong speed).
- Untitled projects no longer share one autosave file; a crash autosave is offered again when you open that project.
- Recent projects keep "&" in their names and follow renames in the Project Manager; Save As names the project after its file.
- Tooltips show the keys you set in Keyboard Customization; Cancel there also removes profiles made in that session.
- Imported clips go into the bin you are looking at; the Audio page timeline starts at the top; clips on locked tracks can't be selected.
- Subtitle files in UTF-16 or Windows encodings import correctly; saving right after typing a title no longer leaves the project marked as changed.

Projects saved by this version (format 17) cannot be opened by older versions.
