Kinoklip 0.2.2-alpha.1 - v2.2: the Cut page and small timeline animations. Still an alpha: expect bugs, keep backups.

## Download and run (Linux x86_64)
1. Download `Kinoklip-x86_64.run`.
2. Make it executable: right-click > Properties > "Is executable", or `chmod +x Kinoklip-x86_64.run`.
3. Double-click it (or run `./Kinoklip-x86_64.run`). The first start unpacks into `~/.cache/kinoklip`; later starts are fast.

Needs: x86_64 Linux, OpenGL 3.3, `zstd` + `tar` (or `bsdtar`). Wayland and X11. `kinoklip-x86_64.tar.zst` is the same app as a portable folder; `SHA256SUMS.txt` has checksums.

## New
- **Timeline animations.** Short animations (about a fifth of a second) make edits easier to follow:
  - after a trim, ripple trim, roll, nudge, ripple delete, close gap, insert, overwrite, append or a Cut page smart edit, the clips that moved slide into their new place;
  - new clips grow in and removed clips fade out; undo and redo slide everything back;
  - a blade cut flashes along the cut and the two halves settle together;
  - a trimmed edge flashes when you let go of the mouse, and the selected edit point pulses once when you select it or trim it from the keyboard;
  - the playhead line glides when it jumps (Up / Down to edit points, Home / End, a click on the Cut page's upper timeline);
  - the snap indicator fades in and out.

  Only the picture moves: the edit itself is done at once, clicks always hit where the clips really are, dragging stays exactly under the mouse and playback is never animated. Turn them off or change their speed in **Preferences > UI Settings > Animate timeline edits / Animation speed**. KDE's global animation speed is respected (set to instant, they are off).
- **Cut page** (Shift+3, or the Cut button between Media and Edit), for fast editing on one timeline:
  - media pool, one large viewer and a tool bar of smart edits above a dual timeline: the upper one always shows the whole timeline, the lower one is zoomed in with the playhead held in its centre;
  - the viewer shows the source clip, the **source tape** (every clip of the current bin back to back, with in / out marks across clips) or the timeline; Q switches;
  - smart edits: **Smart Insert** (at the nearest edit point, so no clip is split), **Append**, **Ripple Overwrite**, **Close Up**, **Place On Top** and **Source Overwrite** (lines up by timecode), each one undo step;
  - edge drags in the lower timeline are ripple trims; blade, ripple delete and a cross dissolve at the nearest edit from the tool bar;
  - a strip view of the media pool (a film strip across each clip) and **Fast Review**, which plays long shots faster.

  Edit and Cut share the same project, timeline, playhead, selection and undo history.

Everything from 0.2.1-alpha.1 is included (opening compound clips by double-click, new tracks by dragging past the outermost one, and all of 0.2.0).

## Known limitations
- Colour grading, compositing and audio pages are not built yet (roadmap v2.5).
- Cut page: the sync bin, boring detector, trim editor window, audio trim view and transitions other than a cross dissolve are not there yet; Close Up zooms on the centre instead of finding faces.
- Mask tracking is not implemented yet.
- Intel VAAPI paths are still unverified on real Iris Xe hardware.
