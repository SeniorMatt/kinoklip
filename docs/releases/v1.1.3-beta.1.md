## [Download Kinoklip 1.1.3 Beta for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v1.1.3-beta.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with Gear Lever (or update it there), or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Beta: keep backups.

**New to Kinoklip? Read the [guide](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/guide/readme.md).**

### New
- **Live Preview in the Effects library**, like Resolve: rest the pointer on an effect, transition, title or generator and the timeline viewer shows the current frame with it applied. Nothing changes in your project until you actually drop or double-click it.
  - Effects preview on the selected clip under the playhead (or the top clip there).
  - Transitions preview on the nearest cut; titles appear over the picture.
  - For transitions and titles, moving the pointer left and right along the entry scrubs through them, so you can see a Scroll title or a dissolve play out.
  - Turn it off with the "..." button in the Effects library title bar > Live Preview.
