## [Download Kinoklip 1.1.4 Beta for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v1.1.4-beta.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with [Gear Lever](https://flathub.org/apps/it.mijorus.gearlever) (or update it there), or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Beta: keep backups.

**New to Kinoklip? Read the [guide](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/guide/readme.md).**

### New
- **Smoother Live Preview of transitions and titles**: moving the pointer along an entry in the Effects library now follows it closely, and a line on the entry shows where in the transition or title you are.
- **Mouse Back / Forward buttons in the Media Pool** go back and forth through the bins you opened, like a file manager.
- **Thumbnail size in the Media Pool**: a slider in the toolbar, or Ctrl+mouse wheel over the clips. Make them smaller to see more clips at once.

### Fixed
- Scrolling the Media Pool thumbnails with the mouse wheel no longer jumps about three rows per notch; it moves a third of a row, and touchpads scroll smoothly.
