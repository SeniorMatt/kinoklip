## [Download Kinoklip 1.1.2 Beta for Linux (AppImage)](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/releases/download/v1.1.2-beta.1/Kinoklip-x86_64.AppImage)

Open it with [Gear Lever](https://flathub.org/apps/it.mijorus.gearlever) (or update it there), or make it executable (right-click > Properties > "Is executable") and double-click it. Beta: keep backups.

**New to Kinoklip? Read the [guide](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/guide/readme.md).**

### New
- **Report a bug with one click**: **Help > Report a Bug or Inconsistency...** opens GitHub's report form in your browser with the Kinoklip, [Qt](https://www.qt.io) and [FFmpeg](https://ffmpeg.org) versions, your distribution, desktop and graphics already filled in. You see exactly that text first, and choose to share it, open the form without it, or copy it for reporting elsewhere. Pick a bug, something that **works differently from [DaVinci Resolve](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)**, or an idea.
- New, short **report forms on GitHub** that ask what you did, what happened and what you expected.
- **Privacy, written down**: Kinoklip collects and sends nothing about you, no telemetry, no analytics, no automatic crash reports. Details in [docs/privacy.md](https://github.com/SeniorMatt/kinoklip/blob/main/docs/privacy.md).

Projects saved by this version (format 17) cannot be opened by versions before 1.1.0.
