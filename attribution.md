The Linux bundles also contain third-party libraries and data, each under its own licence: Qt
(LGPL-3.0), KDE Frameworks and the Breeze widget style and icons (LGPL-2.0/2.1/3.0-or-later),
FFmpeg (built with GPL components, GPL-2.0-or-later / GPL-3.0) and the codec libraries it uses
(x264, x265, dav1d, libaom, SVT-AV1, libvpx, Opus, LAME, Vorbis and others), and the system
libraries they need, the Noto Color Emoji font (SIL OFL 1.1) for emoji in titles and captions, and ONNX Runtime (MIT, with its dependencies protobuf-lite, Abseil and cpuinfo: BSD-3-Clause / Apache-2.0 / BSD-2-Clause), the CPU inference engine of Background Removal.
The full list and every licence text ship inside the bundle under
`usr/share/doc/kinoklip/`.

### Codecs and patents
FFmpeg in the bundles is configured with `--enable-gpl --enable-version3` and without
`--enable-nonfree`, so no non-redistributable component (such as fdk-aac) is included; AAC is
encoded by FFmpeg's own native encoder. H.264 (x264, OpenH264), H.265 / HEVC (x265) and AAC are
covered by patents in some countries. Kinoklip is distributed free of charge as open-source software;
whether a patent licence is needed to use these formats depends on the user's country and use, and
is the user's responsibility. Builds for repositories that exclude patented codecs (for example
Fedora or Flathub) would take these codecs from the system or a codecs extension instead of
bundling them.
